## Korean building footprints — KOGL Type 1

Building footprints around Korean cities (`data/seoul_metro/*_context.json.gz`,
`data/gumi/`, `data/daedeok/` and similar Korean windows) derive from the Ministry of
Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT) GIS Integrated Building Information
(국토교통부 건축물대장 정보), published under
[Korea Open Government License Type 1](https://www.kogl.or.kr/info/license.do)
— source attribution required, commercial use and modification allowed.

## Natural Earth — public domain

World land geometry derives from Natural Earth 1:10m data, which is in the public
domain. Made with Natural Earth. <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/>

## Copernicus land cover — CC BY 4.0

Land-cover rasters derive from Copernicus Global Land Service: Land Cover 100m,
collection 3, epoch 2019 (Buchhorn et al., 2020,
[doi:10.5281/zenodo.3939050](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3939050)),
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Copernicus Service
information 2020.

## Terrain Tiles

Relief and terrain meshes use Terrain Tiles on AWS (Mapzen/Tilezen), whose sources
include NASA SRTM, USGS datasets and ETOPO1. Attribution details:
<https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md>.
